A little teamwork

I wanted to add a bit more variation as the stages progressed, while keeping the same controls and the look of the game. So from stage 16, some of the characters now arrive holding hands. You need to press their keys together to clear them.

The tricky part was making that obvious without adding more text or UI. I went through quite a few versions of the arms before landing on paws and vines that flex when you only press one key, then let go when you get both.

It’s a small addition, but I’m excited to see how it changes the rhythm of the later stages. Also, watching Team Squish send a pair spinning offscreen is pretty fun.
