Wait, what did I just pick up?

Adding the random power-up shuffle was fun, but it created a new problem: once you picked something up, it wasn’t always obvious what you’d actually got. Especially with Flurry, where the change is that any key works.

So the shuffle now gives the selected item a little moment to show itself, and Flurry sends a translucent rainbow circle out from the pickup spot. A bit of a “yep, rainbow time!” moment.

I’m trying to explain these things through the characters, colours and animations instead of adding more words to the screen. Funny how adding a little surprise also means finding a better way to show what just happened.
