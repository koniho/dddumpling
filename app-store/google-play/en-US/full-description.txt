Small dumpling. Big adventure.

DDDumpling is a colorful tap and swipe game full of cute squishy characters, playful boss battles and collectible friends. Match the falling prompts, rescue your run with a swipe, and guide your dumpling through a lively offline arcade adventure.

TAP, SWIPE AND SQUISH

Tap the character keys in the right order before falling sequences reach the bottom. Build your rhythm, react to linked pairs and chase a higher score. Catch a power-up to turn rapid taps into a Flurry, swipe through enemies with Ninja, or send your companion bouncing into action with Team Squish.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY

Start in Adventure and explore Slime Hills, Crystal, Mushroom and underwater lands. Beat the first boss to unlock Survival and begin opening up Boss Time Attack.

• Adventure: clear stages, discover lands and befriend the bosses you defeat.
• Survival: face increasingly wild rainbow waves, choose when to use your power-ups and earn a duck squishy. Survive five minutes for a special friend.
• Boss Time Attack: race the bosses you have befriended, then tackle All Bosses once all four are yours.

Each mode keeps its own records. Review saved runs, improve your accuracy and find your next personal best.

BOSS BATTLES WITH PERSONALITY

These squishy bosses need more than quick taps. Drag Slime's wobbly weak spot, split Dark Divide with two fingers, defend against Octopulse and pull its exposed arms, or shake Fly Agaric's mushroom cap while dodging spores. Learn their gestures and turn defeated bosses into collectible friends.

COLLECT CUTE SQUISHIES

Choose your first squishy companion, then collect dumplings, fruits, candies, curious creatures, ducks and boss friends. Tap a friend in your display case to read its story, watch it react and enjoy its colorful glow rings. Pick a companion to join your next run.

FREE A FRIEND, FOLLOW THE STARS

Adventure's bonus games bring a change of pace. Swipe to open the Steamer and free the squishy inside, then steer along Star Path with both thumbs to gather glowing checkpoints. Earn rewards as your collection grows.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE

Choose Kids Mode for four keys, shorter sequences and gentler bonus challenges, including a Steamer with no timer. Adjust music and sound effects separately, pause during play and return for another run whenever you like.

• Offline play with no ads or in-app purchases
• No account required
• Portrait touch controls with taps, swipes and playful gestures
• Animated squishy collectibles with individual stories
• Separate mode records and detailed run summaries
• Progress, settings and your collection saved on your device
