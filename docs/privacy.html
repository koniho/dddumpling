DDDUMPLING Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This policy explains how DDDUMPLING (Android package com.dddumpling.game) handles information. It covers the initial offline release of the game.

Information used by the game

DDDUMPLING does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or microphone access. It has no advertising or embedded analytics service and does not send your gameplay data to the developer. The game does not request internet access.

The game saves progress and preferences in its private storage on your device. These include your best score, unlocked characters, total collection count, minigame progress, keyboard roster progress, and available game and audio preferences. This information lets the game remember your progress and choices between sessions.

Device backup and narration

Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your backup provider may copy game data to a backup service or transfer it to another device. These system services operate under their providers' privacy policies. The developer does not receive or have access to those backups.

Story narration passes built-in story text to your device's text-to-speech service. That service may process the text locally or through its provider, depending on the selected voice and service settings. DDDUMPLING does not record your voice or provide personal text to the speech service.

Diagnostics and Google Play

The game may display technical error details on your device if an error occurs. It does not automatically upload those details to the developer.

Google Play and Android may independently process installation, device, usage, and diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. Google Play may make app statistics and diagnostic reports available to the developer. These platform services are separate from analytics built into DDDUMPLING. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Support requests

If you contact the developer, the developer receives the contact information and message contents you choose to provide, including any screenshots or technical details you send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate problems. Email or other communication providers process these messages as part of delivering the service. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Saved game data remains on your device until you clear it or remove the app. You can delete the local data through Android Settings, usually under Apps → DDDUMPLING → Storage → Clear storage. This resets your saved progress and preferences. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but system backups may remain and may restore progress after reinstallation. Manage or delete those backups through your device or backup provider's settings.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle your request, resolve related issues, or meet applicable legal requirements. You may contact the developer to request deletion of support correspondence. The developer cannot retrieve or delete game data held only on your device or in your system backups.

Security and sharing

The game uses Android's private app storage for saved progress. DDDUMPLING does not sell personal information or share gameplay data with advertising networks. Access to support correspondence is limited to handling support and related obligations. No storage or communication method can guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask children to provide personal information or create accounts. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer through a support request, contact the developer so it can be reviewed and deleted as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the game's information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Features such as online accounts, cloud progress synchronization, or gameplay analytics would require an updated policy before they are introduced.
