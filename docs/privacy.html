DDDUMPLING Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy explains how DDDUMPLING for Android (package com.dddumpling.game) and iPhone (bundle com.dddumpling.game.ios) handles information.

Which features are in your version

DDDUMPLING does not require a DDDUMPLING account and has no advertising. Released versions that do not include a Firebase configuration do not configure or initialize Firebase Analytics and do not send gameplay analytics. The original offline releases did not include the Analytics SDK.

Versions with optional analytics ask you to choose ALLOW ANALYTICS or NO THANKS before starting Firebase Analytics. Analytics is off until you allow it. Choosing NO THANKS, or closing the initial prompt, keeps it off without affecting gameplay, rewards, or saved progress. You can change the choice later by selecting Privacy on the title screen. This choice is offered worldwide, including in testing releases.

An Android version may separately include Google Play Games cloud saves. That feature is described below and is independent of the Firebase Analytics choice. A build without Play Games configuration continues to save only on the device.

Optional gameplay analytics

If you choose ALLOW in an analytics-enabled version, DDDUMPLING initializes Google Analytics for Firebase and sends fixed event names with a numeric amount. The events measure runs started, finished, or abandoned; stage ranges reached and stages completed; boss attempts, outcomes, and time ranges before first damage; minigame attempts and outcomes; and whether rewards were new or duplicates and where they came from. The amount preserves counter increments and summed boss timing in milliseconds. DDDUMPLING does not send your score, collected character identity, typed keys or words, story activity, settings, name, email address, or support messages as analytics event parameters.

After you allow analytics, Google Analytics also collects information supplied by its SDK, including a random app-instance identifier for that installation, app version, device model, operating system and version, device language, screen and session activity, and app lifecycle events. Google processes IP addresses to derive approximate location such as city, region, and country. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store the IP address.

These records are pseudonymous, not anonymous: the app-instance identifier can connect events from the same installation. Firebase may also process installation identifiers and SDK diagnostic information needed to operate its services. This is separate from uploading game crash reports.

DDDUMPLING disables Android Advertising ID collection, installs the Apple analytics library without IDFA capability, disables Apple IDFV collection, and disables advertising-personalization signals. It does not set a Google Analytics User-ID, send a Play Games or other account ID to Firebase, enable Google Signals, link the Analytics property to advertising products, or use the data for advertising. It does not include Firebase Crashlytics or automatically upload crash reports.

The developer uses analytics to understand aggregate game balance and feature use. Google processes the information to provide Google Analytics under its terms and privacy policy. See Google's Firebase Analytics data disclosure, Google Analytics data safeguards, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Android Play Games cloud saves

In a Play Games-enabled Android version, Google Play Games signs in through the Play Games profile already selected on the device. DDDUMPLING receives the profile's Play Games player ID to keep one installation's local progress bound to the same profile. The player ID is stored only in the app's private local storage and is not sent to Firebase Analytics.

After successful Play Games authentication, DDDUMPLING stores one private cloud-save snapshot with best scores, highest stage, character collection counts, lifetime minigame successes, and cumulative progress counters. It does not save an in-progress run, typed keys or words, settings, or story activity. The current integration does not report gameplay through the Play Games Events service. Google processes the profile, authentication, diagnostics, and saved-game data needed to provide Play Games. You can manage or delete a game's Play Games data through your Play Games profile. See Google's Play Games Services data disclosure.

Information stored on your device

The game saves progress and preferences in its private storage on your device. These include your best score, unlocked characters, total collection count, minigame progress, keyboard roster progress, and available game and audio preferences. Analytics consent and, on Android, the Play Games profile binding are also stored locally so the game can remember your choices.

Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your backup provider may copy game data to a backup service or transfer it to another device. On iPhone, the game saves data in its Application Support container and does not exclude that file from system backup. Apple device backup, restore, or transfer services may therefore copy the saved game according to your device and Apple account settings. The developer does not receive or have access to these system backup copies.

Story narration passes built-in story text to your device's system speech service. That service may process the text locally or through its provider, depending on the platform, selected voice, and service settings. DDDUMPLING does not record your voice or provide personal text to the speech service.

Platform diagnostics and app stores

The game may display technical error details on your device if an error occurs. It does not automatically upload those details to the developer.

Google Play and Android may independently process installation, device, usage, and diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. Google Play may make app statistics and diagnostic reports available to the developer. Apple and iOS may do the same for the App Store. These platform reports are separate from Firebase Analytics and may apply even when you choose NO THANKS. See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support requests

If you contact the developer, the developer receives the contact information and message contents you choose to provide, including any screenshots or technical details you send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate problems. Email or other communication providers process these messages as part of delivering the service. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Retention, choices, and deletion

Saved game data remains in the app's private storage until you clear it or remove the app. On Android, you can delete local data through Settings, usually under Apps → DDDUMPLING → Storage → Clear storage. On iPhone, deleting the app removes its local container. This resets saved progress, preferences, and the analytics choice on that device. System backups may remain and may restore progress after reinstallation; manage or delete those backups through your device or account provider's settings.

If you withdraw analytics permission from the title-screen privacy control, DDDUMPLING stops future Analytics collection, clears Analytics data held locally by the SDK, and resets the app-instance identifier. This does not delete Analytics information already uploaded to Google's servers. If you later choose ALLOW, the SDK creates a new app-instance identifier and collection resumes from that point; declined activity is not sent later.

Before distributing a version with analytics, the developer will set Google Analytics user-level and event-level retention to two months, with renewal on new activity disabled. Google explains that this retention setting does not affect its standard aggregated reports. Aggregate measurements may therefore remain after the user-level retention period.

You may contact the developer to request deletion of Analytics information. DDDUMPLING does not connect Analytics to a name, email address, DDDUMPLING account, or Play Games account, and the game does not expose its app-instance identifier. The developer may therefore be unable to identify which pseudonymous Analytics record belongs to a requester. Where a record can be identified, Google provides user-deletion tools, but Google states that deletion does not remove aggregate data associated with that user.

Play Games cloud data is controlled separately through your Play Games profile. Turning off Firebase Analytics does not delete a Play Games cloud save, and deleting Play Games data does not delete Firebase Analytics history. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle your request, resolve related issues, or meet applicable legal requirements. You may contact the developer to request deletion of support correspondence.

Security and sharing

DDDUMPLING uses platform private storage for local data. Firebase Analytics and Play Games encrypt their collected data in transit. DDDUMPLING does not sell personal information, share gameplay data with advertising networks, or use analytics to track players across other companies' apps or websites. The developer limits analytics access to aggregate product measurement and privacy or deletion work. No storage or communication method can guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask children to provide personal information or create a DDDUMPLING account. Optional Analytics remains off unless ALLOW is selected. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer through a support request, contact the developer so it can be reviewed and deleted as appropriate.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the game's information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. The developer will review the policy and app-store disclosures against each final Android and iPhone release build before distribution.
