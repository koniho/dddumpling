# Age-rating content facts

Use these facts when answering the current App Store Connect questionnaire; Apple determines the rating.

- The game contains recurring stylized, non-realistic fantasy conflict as a core part of play. Falling cartoon characters lunge when they reach the danger line and cost the player one of three lives. Nearly every successful character-key press fires a short abstract glowing shot at a falling character or boss.
- Four cute fantasy bosses are defeated through character-key prompts and touch gestures. Boss attacks include letter-shaped slime bolts, pale letter spores, and tentacle impacts. Visual feedback includes wobbling, stretching, splitting, impacts, flashes, particles, and defeat/reward animations.
- There are no depicted guns, bombs, missiles, bows, swords, knives, or realistic weapons. The FLING powerup does render a bright blade-like line under the player's finger and uses it to slice cartoon characters. It can recur during ordinary play: powerups are offered every 12–20 seconds while eligible, and FLING is one of two initially offered effects and one of three after TEAM SQUISH becomes available. Directional arrows in Starpath and Dark Divide are control hints, not weapons. Starpath has a rocket-like flyer and exhaust, not a weapon.
- There is no blood, gore, realistic injury, graphic death imagery, horror, profanity, sexual content, nudity, alcohol, tobacco, or drugs in the reviewed production scope.
- Minigames award a randomly selected collectible, and duplicate collectibles can occur. Rewards are earned only through gameplay. There are no purchases, paid loot boxes, virtual currency, wagering, cash value, or cash-out mechanism.
- The app has no chat, user-generated content, account creation, social networking, location sharing, advertising, or unrestricted web access. Its only web destination is the fixed privacy-policy link opened in the system browser.
- Built-in character stories can be read aloud with the iPhone system speech synthesizer. The story text is fixed by the app.

## Suggested questionnaire values

- `violenceCartoonOrFantasy`: `FREQUENT`. The presentation is mild, but fantasy attacks and defeats are part of the main play loop rather than isolated scenes.
- `gunsOrOtherWeapons`: `FREQUENT`. No conventional weapon appears, but abstract damaging shots occur throughout normal play and the recurring FLING effect is explicitly blade-like. This is the conservative fit for Apple's broad “weapons, or objects that may cause bodily harm” definition.
- `lootBox`: `false`. Under Apple's purchase-based definition, the free random steamer reward is not a loot box; it cannot be bought.
