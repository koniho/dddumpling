Small dumpling. Big adventure.

Welcome to DDDUMPLING, a colorful arcade game where quick fingers meet a cast of little characters. Tap to clear falling waves, drag through playful encounters, and turn defeated bosses into friends for your collection.

EASY TO START, PLENTY TO MASTER

Match the character keys to the falling sequences before they reach the bottom. Find your rhythm, build combos, and grab powerups that change the action. As the stages get tougher, timing and accuracy make all the difference.

BOSSES WITH PERSONALITY

These bosses demand more than fast tapping. Stretch Slime, split Dark Divide, face Octopulse's reaching tentacles, and shake things up with Fly Agaric. Learn their tells, discover their weaknesses, and earn a cute collectible version of each boss you defeat.

TAKE A PLAYFUL DETOUR

Follow the glowing curves of Starpath or race to pop open the steamer. These quick challenges put a different spin on your reflexes between waves.

A CASE FULL OF FRIENDS

Collect dumplings, fruits, candies, curious creatures, and boss friends. Pan through your shimmering display case, watch your characters come to life, and discover their stories.

MADE FOR LITTLE MOMENTS

Chase a new best score, conquer one more boss, or meet your next favorite character. Enjoy expressive faces, squishy animation, colorful effects, original music, and playful sound.

• Tap-and-drag arcade action
• Four boss battles with distinct mechanics
• Powerups that transform the action
• Starpath and steamer minigames
• An animated character collection
• Offline gameplay with no ads or account required
