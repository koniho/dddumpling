### Little improvements

On the title screen: New lands get a little tour, with clearer trails between stops.

In the release book: Tap outside to close the list. Browse with soft bloops and a little buzz.

### Happy little fixes

Star Path bonus rounds: Your chosen flyer stays with you. The rocket loop ends with one blast-off.

In the display case: Best Score fades away so the heading stays clear.

Please try the new-land tour and travel in both directions. Open the release book, select an entry, go Back, and tap outside the list to close it.

On Star Path, check that your selected flyer remains through retries and rewards. Try both a timed finish and collecting all 20 stars: the rocket loop should stop with a single blast-off, and the finish should complete without a crash.

On the title screen, open and close the display case. Best Score should fade out before the case heading appears and return afterward.

The misc and bug illustrations keep their state after two seconds; they do not automatically reset.
