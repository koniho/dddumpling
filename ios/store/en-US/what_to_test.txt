## Settings, your way

Open Settings from the title screen or during a run. Adjust music and sound effects independently, with separate volume and mute controls. Enable Kids Mode before your next run for slower play, four keys, and shorter words.

## Little improvements

Tap finished results to return to the title. Browse older changes with a yellow scrollbar, clear of the keys.

## Happy little fixes

Zero-hit runs now show 0% accuracy.

Please try Settings from the title and during a run. Adjust and mute each audio channel, check the sound-effects volume preview, and check play pauses and resumes. Enable Kids Mode before starting a new run. Scroll to the oldest release-book entry and check the pane stays above the keys. Finish a run with no hits, check 0% accuracy, and tap the results to return to the title.

The settings, improvements, and bug illustrations do not automatically reset.
