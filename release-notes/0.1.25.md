DDDUMPLING 0.1.25

Your squishy sidekick
- From your very first run: Choose one of five squishies to keep. Your friend waves hello, joins every part of your run, and reacts to your taps and victories.

A little friendly help
- As you discover the game: Your companion teaches each new move when you need it. Enjoy shorter lessons for power-ups, minigames, your display case, and new lands.

Power-up rush
- When a power-up is active: Fling is now Ninja! Busier waves bring airy swishes, sweeping slashes, and chimes from your third combo clear. Flurry clears bloom with rainbows.

Remember that run?
- In your high score list: See your run companion, prizes, and power-ups in richer summaries. Reset scores in settings without losing your squishies or unlocked progress.

Follow the stars
- In Star Path bonus rounds: Each difficulty adds a checkpoint. Blast off with colorful flashes and rings, then enjoy a smoother exit after the last particles fade.

Little improvements
- In your display case: Tap a friend for its story and colorful reactions.
- In the Steamer minigame: Rising steam and a big reveal celebrate your prize.
- Kids Mode and settings: Swipe the Kids Mode slider; enjoy gentler boss projectiles and animated settings.

Happy little fixes
- Returning to Android play: Opening the app again reuses your active game instead of starting another instance.
