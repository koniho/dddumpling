# DDDUMPLING 0.1.26

## Ride the rainbow

After beating the Stage 5 boss: Try Survival! Waves grow wilder, power-ups wait in your rack, and each run earns a duck. Last five minutes for a special friend. Chase your own Survival record!

## Boss Time Attack

After your first Adventure boss: Race bosses you have befriended, or tackle All Bosses once all four are yours. Save best times and explore each battle's accuracy, defense, and damage stats.

## Pick your next adventure

On the title screen: Swipe modes and tap a title or scene to play. Adventure scenery follows your land and companion. A springy LET'S SQUISH welcomes new players; locks explain your next goal.

## Little improvements

- During Adventure: Tap to pause. Rescue Swipes are easier to start and show when spent.
- In Kids Mode: Steamer needs 10 pips with no timer. Projectiles keep their normal speed.
- In your display case: Boss friends sit on the top shelf.

## Happy little fixes

- During Team Squish: Matching prompts nearest the damage line take priority; no match sends your team to the nearest enemy.
- At the end of a stage: A final enemy hit now finishes its damage reaction before the next scene.
