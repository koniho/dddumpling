# DDDUMPLING 0.1.29

## Ready, set, squish!

**Before Boss Time Attack** — Your squishy faces off against the boss with sliding portraits, spoken names and a bouncy new music cue. All-boss runs show your round.

## Welcome to Swampland

**Adventure, stages 21-25** — Meet Gummy Gator! Parry claws and tails, then tap during his opening. Star-marked divebomb words charge when just their final key remains.

## Peek through the fog

**Normal waves in Swampland** — Foggy words reveal one key at a time. Clear the visible key to uncover the next. Revealed keys stay visible after a miss, so keep going!

## Shooting stars

**Adventure, stage 16 onward** — One-key shooting stars race in at double speed. Team Squish can intercept them! They appear 35% less often during power-ups, leaving room for other enemies.

## Super squishy Slime

**In the Super Slime battle** — Meet Super Slime! His first prompt follows a cover cycle. Hits bring worried eyes, gooey sprays and cartoony squishes and boips. Victory leaves a big, dripping splat.

## Little improvements

**During normal waves** — Bigger clear bursts and parting pair hands celebrate each pop.

**Between runs** — Remembered lands and swipeable release pages keep things moving.

**With a physical keyboard** — Use SDF / JKL, or SD / KL for four keys.

## Happy little fixes

**At the damage line** — Last-second hits count until damage lands.

**During boss battles** — Hits react on impact. Time Attack tracks your first hit.

**In iPhone Settings** — Share and review open the App Store.

## Easier to read, more fun to play

Survival and Boss Time Attack run summaries use larger text, including per-boss stats. Octopulse explains early taps, wrong choices and late defenses through companion speech bubbles. Bigger companion props include a flexing cheer rod, and props now follow your squishy through Gummy Gator parries.
