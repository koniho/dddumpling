# DDDUMPLING 0.1.31

Halloween skins bring spooky keys and costumed bosses! Explore Swampland with Gummy Gator, foggy words and shooting-star enemies. Boss Time Attack adds lively versus intros and music. Super Slime gets gooey hits and a dripping defeat. Enjoy bigger pops and summaries, remembered lands, swipeable news, clearer Octopulse feedback, improved companion props, audio controls and keyboard support. Fixes cover last-second hits, boss impact timing, Time Attack stats and iPhone store links.

## Halloween dress-up

In Settings > Skins: Try the Halloween skin! Spooky keys and dressed-up bosses bring a new look with the same squishy gameplay.

## Ready, set, squish!

Before Boss Time Attack: Your squishy faces the boss with sliding panels, bouncy portraits, spoken names and new music. All-boss runs show the round.

## Welcome to Swampland

Adventure, stages 21-25: Meet Gummy Gator! Parry claws and tails, then tap during his opening. Star-marked divebomb words charge when just their final key remains.

## Peek through the fog

Normal waves in Swampland: Foggy words reveal one key at a time. Clear the visible key to uncover the next. Revealed keys stay visible after a miss, so keep going!

## Shooting stars

Adventure, stage 16 onward: One-key shooting stars race in at double speed. Team Squish can intercept them! They appear 35% less often during power-ups, leaving room for other enemies.

## Super squishy Slime

In the Super Slime battle: Meet Super Slime! His first prompt follows a cover cycle. Hits bring worried eyes, gooey sprays and cartoony squishes and boips. Victory leaves a big, dripping splat.

## Little improvements

During normal waves: Bigger pops and parting hands.

Between runs: Remembered lands, swipeable news and bigger summaries.

With your companion: Props follow every leap. Octopulse explains tap mistakes.

Settings and keyboards: Red mute icons; sliders unmute. Keyboard: SDF / JKL or SD / KL.

## Happy little fixes

At the damage line: Last-second hits count until damage lands.

During boss battles: Hits react on impact. Time Attack tracks your first hit.

In iPhone Settings: Share and review open the App Store.
