# DDDUMPLING 0.1.37

Dress up your squishy: tap a pal in your display case to get started. Achievements unlock hats, eyewear, shoes, masks and magical flair. Tap or drag to equip, or shake for a surprise outfit. Every pal keeps their own saved look.

Little improvements: slime banks and cave walls sway, Super Slime makes ripples and has clearer attack sounds, and an occasional invitation after happy milestones lets you share a store review.
