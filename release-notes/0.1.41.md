# DDDUMPLING 0.1.41

## Climb the leaderboards

- **In your high-score lists:** Compare new Survival, Boss Time Attack and clean Adventure runs worldwide. Get a rank alert after your best lands on the board.

## Survival gets a cheer

- **Starting and playing Survival:** Bounce into a rainbow entrance with a spoken welcome. Every 30 seconds, bigger cheers and colorful bursts celebrate your time!

## A little steamer helper

- **In the Steamer mini-game:** Your squishy fans harder as you score, then points when the lid is ready. Glowing prompts make the end of each shuffle clearer.

## Little improvements

- **During tutorials:** An outlined Skip button skips only the current lesson set.
- **Facing Dark Divide:** Keep hitting until the boss flashes vulnerable, then split!

## Happy little fixes

- **During tutorials:** Steamer scenery stays visible, practice has haptics, and the Slime lesson plays once.
- **In your display case:** Swiping back keeps your selected squishy in place.
