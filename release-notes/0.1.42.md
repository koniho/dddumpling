# DDDUMPLING 0.1.42

## Peek, tap, surprise!

- **Adventure scenery:** Spot a hidden visitor in one stage of each land. Tap for 100 bonus points, a happy little dance and a playful sound. Find them all!

## A forest full of life

- **Furry Forest stages:** Follow winding paths and sparkling streams under warm sunbeams. Little critters scamper when you clear an enemy, and bouncy mushrooms greet each stage.

## Swamp under the stars

- **Swampland stages:** Drift through tall reeds beneath a starry sky. Watch frogs, ripples and fireflies that scatter when you clear an enemy.

## Little improvements

- **Entering Adventure lands:** Big welcome titles introduce Crystal Cave, Sea Squish, Furry Forest and Rainbow Run.
- **Across Adventure scenery:** Softer light and color fades brighten the journey. Rainbow Run streams past twice as fast.

## Also new for production players

Previously available in 0.1.41 testing:

- **Climb the leaderboards:** Compare Survival, Boss Time Attack and clean Adventure scores worldwide, with rank alerts.
- **Survival gets a cheer:** A spoken rainbow welcome and growing celebrations every 30 seconds.
- **A little steamer helper:** Your companion fans the Steamer and points to the ready lid; glowing prompts clarify the shuffle.
- **Little improvements:** Skip only the active tutorial set, with clearer Dark Divide guidance.
- **Happy little fixes:** Steamer tutorial scenery, practice haptics, repeated Slime lessons and display-case back swipes are fixed.
