# DDDUMPLING — draft Google Play listing

## App name
DDDUMPLING

## Short description
Tap cute characters, battle squishy bosses, and grow your shimmering collection.

## Full description
Meet your new favorite little dumplings.

DDDUMPLING is an arcade game played on a honeycomb keyboard of colorful characters. Tap the matching faces to clear falling chains before they reach the bottom. Build your rhythm, react to powerups, and discover new ways to play as your run grows.

BATTLE SQUISHY BOSSES
Stretch Slime, split Dark Divide, outsmart Octopulse, and shake Fly Agaric. Each boss has its own playful gestures and a soft body that wobbles with the action. Defeat a boss to welcome its miniature friend into your collection.

DISCOVER LITTLE SURPRISES
Pop open a dim sum steamer, steer through a winding star path, and discover collectible characters. There are 49 characters to find across dumplings, fruit, candy, stars, cubes, and boss friends.

MAKE YOUR COLLECTION SHINE
Pan through a shimmering display case filled with animated characters. Bring a favorite into focus and explore its little story.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
Start with a smaller keyboard, learn through an animated demonstration, and take on increasingly lively challenges. Enjoy original music and sound effects, with optional character-story narration through your device's speech engine.

The game has no ads, in-app purchases, or account login. Your collection, best score, and settings are saved on your device.

## Release notes
First Google Play test build. Includes four gesture-driven bosses, boss collectibles, a freely pannable display case, and Octopulse visual and projectile improvements.
