## Game data
DDDUMPLING stores your best score, collected characters and their collection counts, progress counters, and settings in local app storage. The game does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated servers. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or device identifiers.

The developer does not collect or sell your personal information through the game. Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and Google account settings. These operating-system services are controlled by their providers.

## Optional narration
When you open a character story, the game can pass its fixed story text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The game prefers an available offline English voice. The selected speech engine operates under its provider's settings and privacy policy; the game does not send your score, collection, or personal information with the narration.

## Deleting data
You can clear DDDUMPLING's app storage through Android settings to remove locally stored game data. Manage any operating-system backup separately in your device or account settings. Uninstalling removes the app's local storage, but a system backup may restore it on a later installation.

## Diagnostics
If the game encounters an error, it may show technical diagnostic text on your device. The game does not automatically send crash reports to the developer. If you choose to contact support, the information you include in your message is used to respond to you.
