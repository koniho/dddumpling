# DDDUMPLING privacy policy

The current Android and iOS policy is maintained in [docs/privacy.html](../docs/privacy.html).
It covers optional Firebase Analytics, consent and withdrawal, identifiers and approximate
location, retention and deletion limits, Play Games cloud saves, device backups, and support data.
This replaces the original offline-only draft; do not use that draft for an analytics release.

The September 13, 2026 revision is included in analytics PR #19 for review.
The public policy URL is https://koniho.github.io/dddumpling-privacy/.
Branch changes are not published until they reach main and the publishing workflow succeeds.

Before distribution, verify the console settings and store declarations using
[the analytics checklist](../docs/firebase-analytics.md). See
[privacy publishing](../app-store/privacy-publishing.md) for the publication workflow.
